## Overview

The ClumsyCat site needed to feel more expressive than a typical utility app
without losing clarity. The page uses motion and responsive layout to create a
strong launch experience while keeping the content path direct.

Framer Motion handles the animation layer, but the goal is restraint: motion
should support hierarchy and polish rather than making the page harder to scan.
